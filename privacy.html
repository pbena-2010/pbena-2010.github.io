Privacy policy: History Shorts Uploader

Last updated: 14 November 2026

History Shorts Uploader ("the app") is a private tool used only by the owner of the HistoryAnim YouTube channel, on the owner's own computer. It uses YouTube API Services.

What the app accesses

  • With the owner's permission (Google OAuth), the app uploads videos, titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails to the owner's own YouTube channel.
  • It reads the owner's own channel and video data: video lists, view, like and comment counts, and YouTube Analytics reports (for example watch time, average view percentage and subscribers gained).

How the data is used, stored and shared

  • The data is used only to upload the owner's videos and to show the owner which of their videos perform best.
  • The OAuth access and refresh tokens, and the analytics reports, are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are not sent to any server operated by the app, and the app has no server.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • The app does not collect any information about viewers or about other YouTube users.

Google and YouTube

By using the app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page (Third-party apps & services). The owner can delete the stored tokens and reports by deleting the app's folder on their computer. Stored analytics reports are refreshed daily and are not kept for longer than needed.
