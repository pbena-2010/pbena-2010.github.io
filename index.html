History Shorts Uploader

History Shorts Uploader is a private tool used by the owner of the HistoryAnim YouTube channel. It runs on the owner's own computer and does two things:

  • uploads the channel's own animated history Shorts, with their titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails, through the YouTube Data API;
  • reads the channel's own video statistics and YouTube Analytics reports, so the owner can see which videos perform best.

It is not offered to anyone else, it has no other users, and it does not access any other channel or account.

This app uses YouTube API Services. See the privacy policy and terms of service.
